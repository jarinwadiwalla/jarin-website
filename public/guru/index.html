Morning Digest Email

A short daily email with today's goals, this week's goals, top 3 to-dos, yesterday's misses, and your quarterly anchors. Sent once per day at your chosen UTC hour.

Reference: hour 13 UTC ≈ 6am Pacific / 9am Eastern / 7pm Bali (UTC+8).

Cron setup (one-time)

Cloudflare Pages has no native cron, so add this to an external scheduler (cron-job.org, GitHub Actions, etc.) to hit every hour. The endpoint decides whether it's time to send. 

curl -X POST https://jarinwadiwalla.com/api/intention-digest \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"action":"check"}'

Weekly Review

Step 1 of 3

For each of last week's weekly goals, pick how it went. Goals you mark as partial get suggested as next week's anchors in step 2.

Pick up to 3 weekly goals for the coming week. Each goal can be scoped to a venture.

Add a brief progress note for each active quarterly goal (optional — leave blank to skip).

Quarterly Review

Step 1 of 3

For each of this quarter's goals, pick how it went. Partial goals pre-seed into next quarter so they carry forward; missed goals are dropped from history; hits get closed out as done.

Define up to 5 quarterly goals for the coming quarter. Partial carries from step 1 pre-fill the first rows; edit freely.

Optional closing reflection on goals you marked Hit or Miss in step 1. What worked? What didn't? (Saved as a goal_checkin with today's date.)
